Watchlist Match

Figure out what to watch together. Enter two or more Letterboxd usernames to find the films on everyone's watchlist, sorted by average rating.

Press comma or enter to lock in each name, profile links work too.

What is a Letterboxd watchlist blend?

A watchlist blend compares two or more Letterboxd watchlists and keeps only the overlap, the films that appear on every list. Those are the movies you genuinely all want to watch. No more scrolling through hundreds of posters trying to find common ground.

How do we decide what to watch together?

Movie night, date night, or a long-distance watch party: if you both use Letterboxd, your watchlists already hold the answer. Compare them here and the highest-rated films you share rise to the top, so deciding what to watch tonight takes about five seconds.

How is this different from a watchlist picker?

A watchlist picker pulls one random film from your lists. Watchlist Match shows the full overlap, sorted by Letterboxd average rating, so the best-loved films float to the top and you choose together.

Does it work with private watchlists?

No, only public Letterboxd watchlists can be read. Profiles set to private will show an error. Nothing is stored: your usernames stay in your browser.
