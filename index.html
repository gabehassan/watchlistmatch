Watchlist Match

A free Letterboxd watchlist picker and compare tool. Enter two or more usernames to find the films on everyone's watchlist, sorted by average rating.

Press comma or enter to lock in each name, profile links work too.

Letterboxd watchlist picker for movie night

Watchlist Match is built for the moment when everyone wants to watch something, but nobody wants to scroll through hundreds of films. Add two or more Letterboxd usernames and it becomes a shared watchlist picker: only the films that appear on every public watchlist are shown.

What is a Letterboxd watchlist blend?

A watchlist blend compares two or more Letterboxd watchlists and keeps only the overlap, the films that appear on every list. Those are the movies you genuinely all want to watch. No more scrolling through hundreds of posters trying to find common ground.

How do I compare Letterboxd watchlists?

Enter usernames, @handles, or Letterboxd profile links, then submit. Watchlist Match reads each public watchlist, finds the intersection, and ranks the shared films by Letterboxd average rating with genres shown underneath. It works for couples, roommates, friend groups, and watch parties.

Can I compare more than two Letterboxd users?

Yes. Add up to eight public Letterboxd usernames and Watchlist Match will show the films that appear on every watchlist, which is useful for group movie nights and watch parties.

How do we decide what to watch together?

Movie night, date night, or a long-distance watch party: if you both use Letterboxd, your watchlists already hold the answer. Compare them here and the highest-rated films you share rise to the top, so deciding what to watch tonight takes about five seconds.

How is this different from a watchlist picker?

A watchlist picker pulls one random film from your lists. Watchlist Match shows the full overlap first, sorted by Letterboxd average rating, so the best-loved films float to the top and you choose together. It is a practical alternative when you want a ranked shortlist instead of one random result.

Does it work with private watchlists?

No, only public Letterboxd watchlists can be read. Profiles set to private will show an error. Nothing is stored: your usernames stay in your browser.
